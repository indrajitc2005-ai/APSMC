M-04 / Delivery Reach [Pan-India]

Road freight from West Bengal. Lead times by zone [TBD].

  1. QC-1

    Incoming material inspection (PMI)

    100% Positive Material Identification (PMI) of raw ingots, master alloys and secondary feedstock at receiving staging.

    Method: Handheld XRF Spectrometry (Olympus/Evident Vanta) calibrated against ASTM/IS reference standards.

  2. QC-2

    In-process melt & synthesis control

    Optical emission and UV-Vis spectrophotometry checkpoints for alloy melts, pre-treatment baths and additive concentrations.

    Frequency: Real-time sensor logging & per-batch chemical titration assay.

  3. QC-3

    Pre-dispatch lot certification

    Final multi-point assay verification against customer specification; Certificate of Analysis (CoA) issued with every consignment.

    Retention samples: Archival retention for 180 days with full batch traceability.
